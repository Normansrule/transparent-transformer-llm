2 · Sparse autoencoder: from neurons to features

The 256 neurons of block 2's perceptron layer each respond to a mix of things. A sparse autoencoder re-expresses every activation as a few of 512 learned features. Each one ideally means one thing. This is the dictionary-learning method of published interpretability research, at toy scale.
