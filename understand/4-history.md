## Three patterns in the timeline

1. **Ideas wait for compute.** Backpropagation was known in 1986; it took GPUs and huge datasets around 2012 to make deep networks win. The transformer (2017) is simple; GPT-3 (2020) is the same recipe about a million times larger than this repository's model.
2. **Winters follow hype.** The 1969 critique of perceptrons cooled neural-network research for over a decade. Measuring honestly, as this repository tries to, is the antidote.
3. **The question of minds never went away.** Every leap in capability revives Turing's question. Frame 5 takes it seriously.
